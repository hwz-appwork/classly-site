Privacy Policy

Classly for Android · Last updated: 13 November 2026
Short version: Classly does not collect, transmit, or share any data. The app does not request the INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending information anywhere.

1. What we collect

Nothing. Classly has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, and no third-party libraries that transmit data.

2. Where your data lives

Your classes, homework, exams and settings are stored in a private database on your device, accessible only to the app. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

You can export a copy of your data at any time from Settings → Export data. That file is written to a location you choose on your device; where it goes afterwards is entirely up to you.

3. Permissions we request, and why

  • Notifications — to remind you before a class starts and before a homework deadline.
  • Exact alarms — so those reminders arrive on time rather than whenever the system feels like it.
  • Run at startup — to re-schedule your reminders after you restart your phone.
  • Calendar (optional) — only if you tap “Sync to phone calendar”. The app writes your class times into a calendar on your device so your existing calendar app can show them. It does not read or upload the contents of your other events. Every event it creates is tagged, and “Remove synced classes” deletes only those.

We do not request the internet permission, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access.

4. Children

Classly is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

4a. Calendar sync, in plain terms

If your phone has a Google account signed in, events written into the device calendar are synced to Google Calendar by Android itself — not by this app. Classly has no network access of its own, so nothing is sent anywhere by us.

5. Third parties

There are none. Classly contains no advertising networks, analytics providers, or social media integrations.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date. If a future version of the app introduces a feature that requires network access, it will be stated here before that version is released.
